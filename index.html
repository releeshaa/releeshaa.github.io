About

Backend depth, frontend range, and a habit of owning the feature.

I'm a full stack developer with nearly four years of production experience designing, building and maintaining web applications with Laravel and Vue.js.

Most of that time went into e-commerce: notification systems, abandoned carts, product reviews, exports, and the REST APIs that a mobile app depends on. I also led a team of four, which meant code reviews, mentoring, and sorting out production issues as they appeared.

I learn new tools quickly and I work in Agile teams with Jira.
